# Selection decisions

## Included

| Skill | Existing substantive work | Why it belongs |
|---|---|---|
| handoff | Scope precedence, evidence boundaries, read-only takeover, existing authorization, locked-result handling | Makes a continuation inspectable and reduces opportunities for accidental replay. |
| reviewing-design-documents | Eight-question rubric, root-cause deduplication, evidence and coverage states, deterministic verdict mapping, secondary-question handling | Makes design review findings traceable instead of generating a generic checklist. |
| performance-self-review | Source coverage accounting, personal/team attribution, metric windows, distinction between reported and verified outcomes | Produces a narrative whose claims can be checked against a ledger. |
| simplify-tech-writing | Chinese/English technical adaptations, preservation of conditions and evidence boundaries, 11 original examples | Makes explanations clearer without turning uncertainty into a promise or changing operational meaning. A separate public edition excludes restricted reference material. |

Selection reflects existing methods. New introduction text, translations, or packaging alone do not make a third-party skill a representative work.

## Excluded from this release

| Material | Reason |
|---|---|
| Archify, Diagram Design, Karpathy Guidelines, Stop Slop | Imported third-party capabilities with limited local routing, wording, or packaging changes. Useful upstream projects, but not representative original work for this collection. |
| Other imported skills and adaptations with unclear origin | Original contribution or redistribution basis not sufficiently established for this release. |
| Third-party attachments and systematic standard mappings from the development edition of simplify-tech-writing | Not part of the public skill. Source attribution remains, but the standard PDF, dictionary, page/entry tables and numbered-rule mapping are omitted. |
| Internal integrations and operational collectors | Depend on private context, permissions, or runtime configuration. |
| Personal runtime configuration, account registries, coordination records, archives and historical artifacts | Outside the portable public scope. |
| Reference books, standards PDFs, fan art and other unverified attachments | Rights to redistribute were not established. |

Exclusion is not a judgment of an upstream project's quality or safety. This is a deliberately small initial release.
